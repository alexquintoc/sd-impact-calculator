# C4: LOCAL CULTURE — Extended Guidance

Related criterion: [C4: LOCAL CULTURE](../../generated/criteria/CCP3.md)

## What are some strategies that can be used to adapt a design to a regional culture?
Designing products or services may require the communication design team to learn from a local culture, adapting their design accordingly. Some strategies might include:

* the use of '[Appropriate Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appropriate_technology)'.  

* designing interventions around '[Placemaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Placemaking)' efforts.

* designing [cultural probes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_probe) or similar strategies to engage a local culture.
