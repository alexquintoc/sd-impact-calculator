# CM9: CULTURAL POLICY — Extended Guidance

Related criterion: [CM9: CULTURAL POLICY](../../generated/criteria/CCP5.md)

## What should a cultural sustainability policy document include?

The document should describe the actions taken by an organization in order to promote cultural development in its community. This document might include activities in one or all three areas as described in Terry McKinley’s Creative Empowerment Index:

1) Expenditures on the consumption of cultural products and activities;
2) Expenditures on the creation of new cultural products, services or activities;
3) The number of people that the organization supports to engage in creative activities.
Source: Cultural Indicators of Development. Terry McKinley. United Nations Research Institute for Social Development. 1997. http://unesdoc.unesco.org/images/0012/001241/124176eo.pdf

Other references for cultural sustainability policies include UNESCO’s Convention on the Protection and Promotion of the Diversity of Cultural Expressions. See: http://unesdoc.unesco.org/images/0014/001429/142919e.pdf#page=9

## What are some examples of a cultural sustainability policy document?

One example is the International Finance Corporation’s Overview of Performance Standards on Environmental and Social Sustainability, including its [Performance Standard 8 on Cultural Heritage] (https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2012/ifc-performance-standard-8).

### What constitutes a “publicly-visible policy”?

The policy should be posted on a publicly-accessible website or could be distributed on the project itself.
