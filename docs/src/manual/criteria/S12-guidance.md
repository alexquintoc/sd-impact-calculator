## What is Fair Trade?

“Fair Trade is a trading partnership, based on dialogue, transparency and respect, that seeks greater equity in international trade. It contributes to sustainable development by offering better trading conditions to, and securing the rights of, marginalized producers and workers – especially in the South.” source: http://www.wfto.com/fair-trade/definition-fair-trade (last accessed: January 15, 2016)

## Fair Trade applied to Creatives

A related Fair Trade concept applied to Creative professions is the definition of the Fair Culture Charter defined at UNESCO's Mondiacult 25 Conference in Spain. The eight principles of the Fair Culture Charter include:
1. Decent working conditions and fair remuneration
2. Access to diverse cultural expressions and resources
3. Non-discrimination and gender equality
4. Local development
5. Market access
6. Digital equity and ethics
7. Respect for the environment
8. Public and consumer awareness
