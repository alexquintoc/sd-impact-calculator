# E19: TRANSPORT — Extended Guidance

Related criterion: [E19: TRANSPORT](../../generated/criteria/EEM1.md)

### What kinds of actions may be considered to improve shipping efficiency and reduce transportation and waste?

Sizing boxes and palettes to maximize the number and volume of product shipped, and minimizing associated packaging materials (fill, tissue, foam, label, tape, plastic wrap, etc.) help achieve efficiency and transportation and waste reduction goals.

### What kinds of actions may be considered to improve warehousing efficiency and reduce transportation and waste?

Reducing the need to warehouse materials and completed projects can help achieve efficiency and transportation and waste reduction goals.

‘Just in time’, or [lean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lean_manufacturing), manufacturing is a strategy that reduces the need for warehousing.

Packaging may be designed for more efficient use of space and/or weight reduction.
