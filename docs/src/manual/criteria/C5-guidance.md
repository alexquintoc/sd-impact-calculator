# C5: CULTURAL DIVERSITY — Extended Guidance

Related criterion: [C5: CULTURAL DIVERSITY](../../generated/criteria/CCP4.md)

## What does cultural diversity mean?

[Cultural diversity](https://www.unesco.org/en/query-list/c/culture-diversity) refers to the variety of cultural expressions and practices that exist within a society or among different societies. This diversity encompasses differences in language, customs, beliefs, and values, contributing to a rich tapestry of human experience.

## What type of projects promote or enhance cultural diversity?
Projects that contribute to one or more of UNESCO’s Cultural Policies principles. UNESCO has a [list of projects](https://www.unesco.org/creativity/en/projects) created out of the 2005 Convention on the Protection and Promotion of the Diversity of Cultural Expressions.
