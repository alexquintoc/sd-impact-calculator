# E16: RENEWABLE ENERGY — Extended Guidance

Related criterion: [E16: RENEWABLE ENERGY](../../generated/criteria/EE1.md)

## Purpose

### What is considered project ‘production,’ and what constitutes a ‘majority of the energy?’
The number and type of production steps, and the amount and type of energy needed for each step, will vary for different products. In general, production of a project includes generation of the materials, processing of the materials, and fabrication. You may also consider associated packaging, shipping and distribution of the project as part of the production.

Considering all of the production steps, more than 50% of the energy used must come from renewable energy sources. In some cases it may not be possible to generate renewable sources directly, and therefore purchases of ‘green energy’ may be substituted but must constitute more than 50% of the total energy used.

### What is renewable energy?
Examples of renewable energy sources include solar, wind, geothermal, water, biogas, and biofuel.

See: http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renewable_energy

### What is green energy?
As used in this Criterion, ‘green energy’ refers to the purchase of energy that has been produced with renewable sources. For example, green energy may be purchased from an energy supplier or distributor.
