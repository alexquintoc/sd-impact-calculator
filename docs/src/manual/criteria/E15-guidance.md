### What is thermal treatment?

Thermal treatment refers to the incineration of waste and/or the incineration of waste for energy recovery.

### What is local?

For the purpose of this criterion, ‘local’ refers to a facility or system directly and easily accessible to the user. While there is no standard for distance, 100 miles is often used as the distance within which ‘local’ agricultural products may be sourced.
