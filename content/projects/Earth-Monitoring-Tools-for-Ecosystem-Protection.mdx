Services like [Planet ](https://www.planet.com/)provide frequent, high-resolution Earth observation data for tracking forest clearing, land-use change, and ecosystem health, while initiatives such as Wilderness Society’s [Watch on Nature](https://wilderness.org.au/) use near real-time imagery and citizen participation to expose deforestation. These tools show how communication design can make environmental change visible, actionable, and accountable—helping communities, advocates, and institutions respond faster to threats on the ground.
