**Translation Commons** is a nonprofit language-technology initiative working to make sure that more communities can use their languages fully in digital spaces. Through projects such as the Language Digitization Initiative, Translation Commons provides open resources, guidelines, training, and tools that support Indigenous and marginalized language communities in bringing their languages online. The project is a strong example of design and technology serving cultural sustainability: it helps preserve endangered languages, expands access to information, supports linguistic diversity, and creates shared knowledge resources that communities can adapt for their own needs.

From an SD Standard perspective, Translation Commons connects especially well with criteria related to **Endangered Languages, Cultural Diversity, Accessibility, Open Intellectual Property, Education**, and **Social Gaps**, showing how communication systems can help protect cultural identity while increasing participation in digital life.
