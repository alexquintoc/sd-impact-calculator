Released in 2021 and implemented the following year, this standard applies to items such as textbooks, workbooks, examination papers, preschool reading materials, classroom lighting, reading/writing desk lamps, and teaching multimedia products.

The standard addresses the design and production of textbooks, workbooks, examination papers, preschool reading materials, classroom lighting, desk lamps, and teaching multimedia products. By treating visual health as a shared responsibility across publishing, product design, lighting, education, and procurement, the policy shows how communication materials can be regulated not only for content, but also for their physical and sensory impact on young audiences.
