# Baseline Scope

This starter baseline describes a common roll-up banner used at events, conferences, and temporary displays.

From studies on PVC banners and aluminum display products, typical findings:

Material production dominates impact (often 50–80%)

PVC is a major environmental burden

Toxic additives (plasticizers, stabilizers)

End-of-life challenges

Aluminum has high upfront impact but good recyclability

Short lifespan = worst-case scenario

## Use Notes

Treat all quantitative values as assumptions.

Replace estimates with supplier, transport, and disposal data before using this as evidence.

Keep SD Standard criteria as IDs only in frontmatter.

## Design Improvement Opportunities

Service models (renting display systems)

Modular reuse systems (swap graphics, keep base)

Material innovation (PVC-free substrates)

Digital substitution (when appropriate)
